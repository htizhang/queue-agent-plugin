## Archived comparison

The reported objective is the arithmetic mean of run-level mean whole-network
flow times. The experiment used 27 fixed configurations across three reentrant
network layouts. The layouts had 2--4 stations, three product classes, 4--6
recipes, and two or three bottleneck visits per job. The design varied service
and arrival variability, processing-load target, setup scale, setup-family
contrast, and setup asymmetry.

After policy development and baseline calibration, the comparison used eight
previously unused workload seeds on those same development-designed
configurations: 27 configurations by 8 seeds, or 216 paired blocks. These were
fresh stochastic realizations, not unseen layouts. Each run excluded 400
arrivals from scoring, measured the next 1,500, admitted 1,200 more, and then
drained the network. Policies in a paired block received identical arrivals,
classes, and service requirements.

The public setup-aware comparators were mean-information adaptations of rules
documented by [Xanthopoulos et al. (2016), Table 2 and
Eq. (7)](https://doi.org/10.5267/j.ijiec.2016.2.002). The normalized PR rule was
an explicit adaptation. Its parameter \(\theta=16\), and raw-time PR's
\(\theta=5\), were selected on development seeds before the comparison below.
Each reduction is `100 * (1 - candidate_mean / comparator_mean)`; a positive
value favors the discovered policy over the comparator in that row.

| Policy | Mean flow time | Candidate reduction |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| **Congestion-priced lookahead** | **192.84** | -- |
| PR, normalized, \(\theta=16\) | 204.95 | 5.91% |
| PR, raw time, \(\theta=5\) | 206.90 | 6.79% |
| SPTNS | 209.80 | 8.08% |
| MMS | 217.82 | 11.47% |
| FCFSNS | 242.72 | 20.55% |
| SPSU | 268.20 | 28.10% |
| Least remaining route work | 1,183.60 | 83.71% |
| SEPT | 1,284.30 | 84.98% |
| WORK | 1,739.62 | 88.91% |
| FCFS | 1,810.78 | 89.35% |

Against normalized PR, the aggregate reduction was **5.91%**, with a paired
95% bootstrap interval of **4.67--7.06%**. The policy had lower seed-averaged
mean flow in **27 of 27 configurations** and lower flow in **199 of 216**
individual paired blocks. The interval used 20,000 resamples of the eight whole
seed vectors while retaining all configurations and policies within each
vector. It quantifies workload randomness conditional on this fixed design.

## Archived mechanism checks

Each ablation changed one component of the frozen policy on the same 216
blocks. Positive values mean the complete policy had lower aggregate mean flow.

| Ablation | Full-policy reduction | Paired 95% interval |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Use uninflated setup costs, \(\beta=1\) | 29.68% | 22.93--35.73% |
| Use current-operation duration instead of \(w_j\) in the planning clock | 1.84% | 1.25--2.51% |
| Fix the setup multiplier at \(\beta=8\) | 0.89% | 0.33--1.59% |
| Reduce search depth from three jobs to one | 0.73% | 0.34--1.18% |
| Remove the closing cost | 0.68% | 0.36--0.99% |

These are conditional comparisons, not an additive decomposition. The
\(\beta=8\) result does not establish the best fixed multiplier. Replacing the
policy at setup-free stations with SEPT left all 216 scores unchanged. Removing
the 0.10 other-station term improved aggregate mean flow by about 0.19%; the
term remains in the implementation to preserve the evaluated policy.

## Fresh migration smoke

Run:

```text
python3 discovery-lab/congestion-priced-lookahead/implementation/smoke_test.py
```

The check needs Python 3.10+ with only the standard library. It verifies:

- a hand-calculated queue where the residual closing cost selects the correct
  recipe orientation;
- exact first-update tracker values;
- remaining work from later returns at a setup-free station;
- FIFO tie-breaking; and
- fresh tracker state from each policy factory.

During migration, these fixtures were also run against the archived candidate
instantiated with `close=1.0, close_basis="rest"`; decisions and tracker state
matched. This is a source-regression check, not a fresh performance experiment.
